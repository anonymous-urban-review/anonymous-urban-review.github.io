02 Abstract

High-quality urban data is essential for city-scale 3D vision, simulation, and embodied perception, yet real-world collection remains expensive, time-consuming, and difficult to annotate at scale. Existing synthetic datasets improve controllability and annotation efficiency, but often rely on fixed maps, limited scene diversity, or task-specific data generation pipelines. In this paper, we present InfiniteUrban, a unified framework for controllable urban scene generation and multi-channel dataset construction. InfiniteUrban consists of four stages: (1) structured parameter parsing from high-level user descriptions, (2) constraint-aware road layout synthesis based on a Wave Function Collapse-Tiling Generator (WFC-TG), (3) hierarchical procedural urban scene construction with documented PCG modules, and (4) automated multi-view data acquisition with synchronized multi-channel rendering. The framework supports controllable generation over road topology, building profiles, environmental conditions, and dynamic scene elements, while producing 13 pixel-aligned channels from aerial and street-level viewpoints. Based on this framework, we build Urban1K, a benchmark subset for evaluating view synthesis and urban perception tasks. Experiments on Urban1K show that the generated data provides effective supervision for neural rendering, depth estimation, normal prediction, and semantic segmentation, and exhibits promising transfer to real-world benchmarks.
